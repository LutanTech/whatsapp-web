How to Link

  1. Open WhatsApp on your phone.
  2. Tap Menu / Settings → Linked Devices.
  3. Tap Link a Device.
  4. Select "Link with phone number instead" at the bottom.
  5. Enter the 8-character code generated on this page.
Keep your phone connected to the internet while completing the setup.
